PHASE 24 — positions only: strip prices, measure the floor
===========================================================
User: "lets commit and start a new phase, remove prices, just emit position by
symbol and position by account&symbol and measure"

PIPELINE
  Trades only. Prices not consumed. Outputs: position-by-account-ticker and
  position-by-ticker. Removed: price source, current-price reduce, both joins,
  both MV sinks. No dedup (unique publisher + exactly-once, from Phase 23).
  100 symbols; partitions 2 then 4; both engines.

WHAT IT ANSWERS
  1. What the price path actually costs, at the same trade rate.
  2. Where the floor is -- position-only is the simplest correct topology.
  3. Whether the earlier bottleneck survives. The trace named
     SinkUpsertMaterializer on the MV path; removing that path should remove it.
     If throughput does NOT improve, that operator was not the limit and the
     investigation was chasing a symptom.

PRE-DECLARED INTERPRETATION
  throughput rises materially -> the price path was the cost
  throughput unchanged (~4,000/s) -> constraint is the trade path or the
                                     environment, and the materializer hunt was
                                     chasing a symptom
  utilization still ~31% -> that number is independent of topology, which would
                            be the most interesting outcome of the three

CARRIED IN FROM PHASE 23
  Trade dedup removed from both engines; generator no longer injects duplicates;
  validator asserts uniqueness instead of requiring duplicates.
  The PRICE operator Deduplicate(keep=[LastRow], order=[ROWTIME]) is a different
  thing and stays -- it is the current-price reduce, not trade dedup.
  Every backpressure figure before Phase 23 was wrong: the /backpressure endpoint
  read 0.0% on a job that was 99.9% blocked. Use per-subtask metrics.
