   name failed with ConcurrentModificationException while one retry
   succeeded server-side without Terraform knowing -> split-brain. Fixed
   with terraform import + reconcile apply.
5. zsh trivia: "$ECR:latest" invokes the :l history modifier — use
   "${ECR}:latest".
